本隐私政策适用于《方块叠叠乐》（Block Stacking Fun），由龙球工作室（Dragon Ball Studio，以下简称"我们"）开发与发布。这是一款完全离线的休闲小游戏——它不收集任何数据，也不需要我们为你做任何记录。

一、零数据收集

本应用无需注册或登录，全程可离线游玩。我们不收集：

  • 姓名、电话、电子邮箱等身份信息
  • 位置信息、通讯录、照片、麦克风、摄像头数据
  • 设备标识符、广告标识符（IDFA）
  • 使用行为埋点或遥测数据

应用内没有广告，不接入任何第三方广告、分析、社交或追踪 SDK，也没有任何联网排行榜。

二、仅存储在你设备本地的数据

你的最高分、对局记录与游戏设置仅保存在设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器——我们既无法访问，也无法读取。更换设备时，数据仅随你自行开启的 Apple 系统级备份（如 iCloud 备份）迁移，该过程由 Apple 管理并加密。

三、系统权限

本应用不请求任何系统权限，断网也能畅玩。

四、未成年人隐私

本应用不收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。没有任何第三方内容或外链。

五、数据的删除

卸载本应用时，设备上的全部本地数据将被一并清除。我们的服务器上不存在任何可供删除的你的数据。

六、政策更新

若本政策发生变化，我们会更新本页面顶部的「更新日期」，重大变更会通过官网或应用内提示的方式告知你。

七、联系我们

如对隐私政策有任何疑问，请发送邮件至 support@blackant.studio，我们将在 15 个工作日内回复。

This Privacy Policy applies to Block Stacking Fun, developed and published by Dragon Ball Studio ("we"). It is a fully offline casual game — it collects no data and keeps no records about you.

1. Zero Data Collection

No sign-up or sign-in is required, and the game works entirely offline. We do not collect:

  • Name, phone, email or other identifying information
  • Location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data
  • Device identifiers or ad identifiers (IDFA)
  • Usage analytics or telemetry

The app has no ads, integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, social or tracking SDKs, and has no online leaderboards.

2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

Your high scores, game history and settings are stored solely on your device and never uploaded — we can neither access nor read them. When switching devices, data only migrates through Apple's own backup mechanisms (e.g. iCloud Backup) that you enable; that process is managed and encrypted by Apple.

3. System Permissions

The app requests no system permissions at all and is playable with the network turned off.

4. Children's Privacy

The app collects no information from anyone, including children. There are no third-party contents or external links.

5. Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app erases all local data on your device. No data of yours exists on our servers to be deleted.

6. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and notify you via this website or an in-app notice for material changes.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email support@blackant.studio — we reply within 15 business days.
