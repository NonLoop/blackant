本隐私政策适用于《恋语日记》（Lianyu Diary），由龙球工作室（Dragon Ball Studio，以下简称"我们"）开发与发布。日记属于两个人之间最私密的内容，因此我们把「一切只留在你们的设备上」作为本应用的第一原则。

一、我们看不到你的任何日记内容

你与 TA 记录的文字、照片、纪念日、树洞悄悄话等全部内容，仅保存在设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器。我们既无法访问，也无法读取它们——这是我们给出的最直接的隐私承诺。

二、我们不收集你的个人信息

本应用无需注册或登录。我们不收集姓名、电话、电子邮箱、位置、通讯录，也不使用设备标识符或广告标识符（IDFA）进行追踪。应用内没有广告，不接入任何第三方广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

三、系统权限说明

  • 照片权限：仅在你为日记添加照片时读取你选择的图片；照片会复制保存进应用本地数据，我们不会将其传送到任何服务器。
  • 通知权限（可选）：仅用于纪念日提醒。你可以在系统设置中随时关闭，关闭后仅影响提醒功能。

除上述权限外，本应用不请求任何其他系统权限。

四、设备安全建议

由于内容保存在本地，请为你的设备设置锁屏密码或 Face ID，并谨慎借用给他人。更换设备时，只有在你自行开启 Apple 系统级备份（如 iCloud 备份）时数据才会迁移，该过程由 Apple 管理并加密。

五、未成年人隐私

本应用面向恋爱关系中的成年用户设计，不面向儿童收集任何信息。

六、数据的删除

你可以随时在应用内删除任意一篇日记或全部记录；卸载本应用时，设备上的全部本地数据将被一并清除。我们的服务器上不存在任何可供删除的你的数据。

七、政策更新

若本政策发生变化，我们会更新本页面顶部的「更新日期」，重大变更会通过官网或应用内提示的方式告知你。

八、联系我们

如对隐私政策有任何疑问，请发送邮件至 support@blackant.studio，我们将在 15 个工作日内回复。

This Privacy Policy applies to Lianyu Diary, developed and published by Dragon Ball Studio ("we"). A diary is the most intimate content between two people, so "everything stays on your devices" is the first principle of this app.

1. We Cannot See Your Entries

All content you and your partner record — text, photos, anniversaries and private notes — is stored solely on your device and never uploaded to our servers. We can neither access nor read it. That is our most direct privacy promise.

2. We Do Not Collect Personal Information

No sign-up or sign-in is required. We do not collect names, phones, emails, locations or contacts, and use no device/ad identifiers (IDFA) for tracking. The app has no ads and integrates no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. System Permissions

  • Photo Library: used only to read images you deliberately pick when adding photos to an entry. Selected photos are copied into the app's local data and never sent to any server.
  • Notifications (optional): used only for anniversary reminders. You may turn it off in System Settings anytime; this only affects reminders.

No other system permissions are requested.

4. Device Security Advice

Since content lives locally, please keep a passcode or Face ID on your device and avoid lending it to others. When switching devices, data only migrates through Apple's own backup mechanisms (e.g. iCloud Backup) that you enable; that process is managed and encrypted by Apple.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is designed for adult users in romantic relationships and does not collect information from children.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete any entry or all records in the app at any time; uninstalling erases all local data on the device. No data of yours exists on our servers to be deleted.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and notify you via this website or an in-app notice for material changes.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email support@blackant.studio — we reply within 15 business days.
