本隐私政策适用于《迷盒星球》（Mystery Box Planet），由龙球工作室（Dragon Ball Studio，以下简称"我们"）开发与发布。请使用本应用前仔细阅读本政策。

一、我们不收集你的身份信息

本应用无需注册或登录。我们不收集你的姓名、电话、电子邮箱、位置、通讯录，也不使用设备标识符或广告标识符（IDFA）进行追踪。应用内没有广告，不接入任何第三方广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

二、仅存储在你设备本地的数据

下列内容仅保存在你自己的设备中：

  • 谜团内容的浏览进度与收藏
  • 你的投票记录与应用偏好设置

三、联网功能与匿名数据

加载未解之谜内容库、同步投票汇总以及浏览探秘社群需要网络连接。相关请求均为匿名访问，我们只处理内容本身与汇总后的投票计数，不会将其与你的个人身份关联。

四、探秘社群（用户发布内容）

若你选择在社群中发帖或回帖，你发布的昵称与内容将对其他用户可见。请不要在帖子中包含你的个人身份信息、联系方式或敏感内容。你可以随时删除自己发布的内容；如需协助，也可通过下方邮箱申请移除。

五、系统权限

本应用仅使用网络访问能力；如你开启通知，我们只会推送新内容上线等提醒。你可以在系统设置中随时关闭，关闭后仅影响对应功能。

六、未成年人隐私

本应用不面向儿童收集任何信息。我们建议未成年人在监护人指导下使用社群功能。

七、数据的删除

卸载本应用将清除设备上的全部本地数据；你在社群中发布的内容可自行删除，或邮件通知我们处理。

八、政策更新

若本政策发生变化，我们会更新本页面顶部的「更新日期」，重大变更会通过官网或应用内提示的方式告知你。

九、联系我们

如对隐私政策或数据处理有任何疑问，请发送邮件至 support@blackant.studio，我们将在 15 个工作日内回复。

This Privacy Policy applies to Mystery Box Planet, developed and published by Dragon Ball Studio ("we"). Please read it before using the app.

1. We Do Not Collect Identifying Information

The app requires no sign-up or sign-in. We do not collect your name, phone, email, location or contacts, and we use no device or ad identifiers (IDFA) for tracking. The app contains no ads and integrates no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following is stored solely on your own device:

  • Browsing progress and favorites across mystery topics
  • Your vote records and app preferences

3. Network Features & Anonymous Data

Loading the mystery library, syncing aggregated vote counts and browsing the explorer community require an internet connection. These requests are anonymous — we handle content and aggregated vote tallies only, never linked to your identity.

4. Explorer Community (User-Generated Content)

If you choose to post in the community, your chosen nickname and content will be visible to other users. Please do not include personal identifying information, contact details or sensitive content in your posts. You can delete your own posts at any time, or contact us by email to have them removed.

5. System Permissions

The app only uses network access; if you enable notifications, we push reminders about new content. You may turn either off in System Settings at any time — doing so only affects the related feature.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect information from children. We recommend minors use the community features only under guardian supervision.

7. Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app erases all local data on your device; community posts you made can be deleted by you, or removed upon email request.

8. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and notify you via this website or an in-app notice for material changes.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or data handling? Email support@blackant.studio — we reply within 15 business days.
