本隐私政策适用于《恋爱话术秘籍》（Love Talk Secrets），由龙球工作室（Dragon Ball Studio，以下简称"我们"）开发与发布。请把感情安心交给这款应用——你的数据不会离开你的设备。

一、我们不收集你的个人信息

本应用无需注册或登录。我们不收集姓名、电话、电子邮箱、位置、通讯录，也不使用设备标识符或广告标识符（IDFA）进行追踪。应用内没有广告，不接入任何第三方广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

二、仅存储在你设备本地的数据

下列内容仅保存在你自己的设备中：

  • 你录入的纪念日（相识日、表白日、周年等）
  • 恋爱日记打卡的文字与图片记录
  • 话术收藏与应用偏好设置

这些数据不会上传到我们的服务器——我们既无法访问，也无法读取。更换设备时，数据仅随你自行开启的 Apple 系统级备份（如 iCloud 备份）迁移，该过程由 Apple 管理并加密。

三、系统权限说明

  • 通知权限（可选）：仅用于纪念日提前提醒，可随时在系统设置中关闭。
  • 剪贴板：使用「一键复制」时，话术文本仅写入你的系统剪贴板，由系统本地处理，我们不做任何上传或记录。

除上述外，本应用不请求照片、位置、麦克风等其他系统权限。

四、第三方服务

本应用不接入第三方登录、社交、分析或广告服务。你在 App Store 的购买行为由 Apple 处理，我们仅能获知购买状态，无法获取你的支付账号等细节。

五、未成年人隐私

本应用面向成年用户设计，不面向儿童收集任何信息。

六、数据的删除

你可以随时在应用内删除任意纪念日或日记记录；卸载本应用时，上述本地数据将被一并清除。我们的服务器上不存在可供删除的你的个人数据。

七、政策更新

若本政策发生变化，我们会更新本页面顶部的「更新日期」，重大变更会通过官网或应用内提示的方式告知你。

八、联系我们

如对隐私政策有任何疑问，请发送邮件至 support@blackant.studio，我们将在 15 个工作日内回复。

This Privacy Policy applies to Love Talk Secrets, developed and published by Dragon Ball Studio ("we"). Rest easy about your love life — your data never leaves your device.

1. We Do Not Collect Personal Information

No sign-up or sign-in is required. We do not collect names, phones, emails, locations or contacts, and use no device/ad identifiers (IDFA) for tracking. The app has no ads and integrates no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following is stored solely on your own device:

  • Anniversaries you enter (first meeting, confession day, yearly milestones)
  • Love diary check-ins — text and image records
  • Favorite scripts and app preferences

This data never reaches our servers — we can neither access nor read it. When switching devices, data only migrates through Apple's own backup mechanisms (e.g. iCloud Backup) that you enable; that process is managed and encrypted by Apple.

3. System Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): used only for advance anniversary reminders; can be turned off in System Settings anytime.
  • Clipboard: the one-tap copy feature only writes script text into your system clipboard, handled locally by the OS — we never upload or log it.

No other permissions (Photos, Location, Microphone, etc.) are requested.

4. Third-Party Services

No third-party sign-in, social, analytics or advertising services are integrated. App Store purchases are handled by Apple; we only see purchase status, never payment details.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is designed for adult users and does not collect information from children.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete any anniversary or diary record in the app at any time; uninstalling erases all local data. No personal data of yours exists on our servers to be deleted.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and notify you via this website or an in-app notice for material changes.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email support@blackant.studio — we reply within 15 business days.
